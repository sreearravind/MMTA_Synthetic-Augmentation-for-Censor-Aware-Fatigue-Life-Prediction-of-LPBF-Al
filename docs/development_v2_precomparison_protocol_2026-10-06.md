## Frozen material and question

Inputs are the [199-row v2 cohort](../data/cohorts/development_v2_199_2026-10-06.csv) (Git blob `ee9f6451e60b290c89c44f681b471b3c007c6f74`), [row-level fold assignments](../data/validation/development_v2_grouped_fold_assignments_2026-10-06.csv) (blob `a4969bc3c6562036a5a980ca248ba78c5359fcb`), and [fold feasibility](../data/validation/development_v2_grouped_fold_feasibility_2026-10-06.csv) (blob `037337e85e5e7d36cb93a22ec8b4af41e2209e08`). Check byte/blob identity and row IDs before fitting. Exact first-exposure experimental observations alone form the evaluation set; do not add graph readings, retests, repeated publications, or synthetic observations to its counts.

The **primary** comparison is the 192 `R=0.1` rows (166 failures, 26 runouts), holding out an entire publication in turn: Wu 41 (33/8), Chen 18 (18/0), and Matušů 133 (115/18). All 11 Matušů treatment series and three platforms remain in its one publication fold. A model trained on all 192 primary rows may subsequently be scored on Romano's seven `R=-1` records (6/1) as a **secondary, unseen-ratio challenge**, separately from the primary result. No Romano outcome selects a model or tuning rule. The previously scored Hamidi Nasab campaign is development evidence; Strauß–Löwisch and Kempf remain reserved and unscored.

Target: predictive distribution of first-exposure `z=log10(N)` at nominal stress amplitude `x=log2(σa/100 MPa)`. A failure contributes a density at its observed `z`; a runout contributes survival probability at its recorded lower bound `c=log10(N_stop)`. Source/publication IDs enter grouping and source effects, never as ordinary transportable dummy predictors. Stress basis, `R`, surface and thermal labels remain audit fields. No fitted `R` or process-effect coefficient is identifiable across these three publication folds.

## Predeclared comparison arms

| ID | Training observations | Model and purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C0** | Real exact training rows only | Pooled censored Weibull AFT E0; simple experimental-only control. |
| **C1** | Same real exact rows | Source-marginal censored Weibull AFT M1 with fixed `τ=0.25`; **primary experimental-only comparator and teacher**. |
| **S0** | Same real rows plus conventional source-preserving synthetic episodes | M1 student, all training publications whose article documents a source-wide `10^7)-cycle stopping policy; fixed count, seed and effective weight. This is the equal-generation self-distillation control. Chen's methods specify that stop even though the selected 18 records contain no observed runout. |
| **S1** | Same real rows plus a subset of the *same generated episodes* | M1 student with a fixed censor-support rule: retain only sources that also contain at least one **real training runout at the documented source stop**. Wu and Matušů are eligible when in training; Chen is ineligible. This is a conservative selection feasibility arm, **not yet a novel learned algorithm**. |

Refit all arms separately on each outer training set. The E0/M1 parameterization, parameter bounds, deterministic multistarts, source marginalization, and 161-node Gauss–Hermite quadrature with 321-node numerical check follow [the existing M1 implementation](../scripts/fit_m1_source_aware.py). The original script's hard-coded 66-row hash/folds are **not** reused; v2 requires an adaptation that reads the frozen v2 files and `publication_group_id`. Fix `τ` at 0.25 for every M1 arm, without optimizing it on outer test outcomes. Sensitivity fits at `τ=0.10` and `0.50` for C1 and S1 are descriptive and cannot replace the primary fit. C0 uses no source intercept.

## Training-only synthetic contract

1. For each outer fold, fit the **C1 teacher solely to original real training outcomes**, including genuine failures and runouts. For each training publication with a documented common stop, calculate the posterior over its source intercept conditional on that publication's real rows and the fitted teacher. In each replicate, sample **one intercept per publication**, shared by its generated episodes. Do not condition on test stress, test life, or external-source outcomes.
2. Generate exactly `n_s` episodes for each eligible publication `s`, where `n_s` is its number of original training rows. Sample a real training stress with replacement **within its recorded condition series**; keep publication, platform, series, stress basis and `R` provenance. Wu/Chen can use their corresponding audited smooth-series labels; Matušů must preserve its 11 workbook series. Sample latent life from the teacher Weibull conditional on the sampled stress and common source intercept. Observe failure if latent `N ≤ 10^7`; otherwise record a right-censored `10^7)-cycle bound. Retain the sampled latent value only in a segregated generator audit, never as an observed outcome. Do not force an event fraction or extrapolate stresses beyond a measured training series.
3. Predeclare seeds `13, 29, 47, 71, 101`; each seed is a stochastic training replicate, **not an independent test cohort**. For S0 and S1, use the identical source-specific draws for the same fold and seed; S1 merely sets Chen episode weights to zero. If a source has no defensible common stop, generate nothing for it and record why. Romano's one specimen-specific 8,889,311-cycle bound does not define a source-wide stopping rule.
4. Give every real row weight 1. Synthetic rows stay in their original publication's integrated likelihood group; assign each generated row weight `2/n_s`, so each source has **at most two effective synthetic contributions**, independent of its sample size. In S1, ineligible sources have zero synthetic weight. Do not count pseudo-rows as independent publications or experimental specimens. Do not use graph rows in the primary fits or a graph-reading interval as a probabilistic life kernel.
5. Fit an M1 student by integrating one common publication offset over the **sum of its real and weighted synthetic conditional log-likelihoods**, rather than integrating each pseudo-row as a new study. Teacher parameters, posterior offsets, preprocessor, random draws and student fit are regenerated within each outer training fold. Check for finite generated lives and likelihoods, valid event/bound operators, in-support stresses, missing/duplicate IDs, train/test publication disjointness, optimizer convergence and quadrature agreement. An invalid replicate is reported with reason and its predeclared seed is never quietly replaced.
6. Save a separate generator ledger (`synthetic=true`, fold, seed, source, series, sampled stress, latent draw, observed event/bound, stop and weight), fitted parameters, per-row real-test predictions, input hashes and software versions. Generated data cannot enter an outer test set or the experimental master. No gate threshold, seed, synthetic count, source eligibility, `τ`, or weight cap is chosen from outer test outcomes. A `5)-per-source cap may be examined only as a labelled later sensitivity and cannot supersede the locked `2)-cap result.

This generator is a parametric self-distillation/weighting intervention: it creates **no new physical fatigue evidence**. S1's observed-runout condition is a fixed support filter and may prove unhelpful; it is not a trained utility gate.

## Scoring and reporting lock

For real test record `i`, let `δ_i=1` denote failure and `δ_i=0` a runout. The primary per-record proper censored score is
`L_i = -δ_i log f_Z(z_i|σa_i) - (1-δ_i) log S_Z(c_i|σa_i)`,
using natural logarithms and density **with respect to log10 cycles**. For M1, integrate a **fresh** `b_new ~ N(0,τ²)` in each predictive failure density or runout survival probability before taking the log; do not infer a held-out publication offset from any of its outcomes. C0 sets `b_new=0`. Use the same recorded runout bound across arms, never treat it as a failed life. Numerical predictive integration at 161 versus 321 nodes must agree to `10^-5` in log score or the fit is flagged.

**Primary summary:** mean `L_i` within each of the three held-out publications, then an unweighted mean of those three publication means (macro NLL; lower is better). Show the pooled specimen-weighted mean separately because Matušů supplies 133/192 rows. For every arm, report the Wu/Chen/Matušů fold means, exact test `n` and event counts, and paired per-row `ΔL_i` relative to C1. Report S1−C1, S0−C1 and S1−S0 fold and macro differences. For S0/S1, report every fixed seed, then the arithmetic mean and minimum–maximum across the five seed-level *macro* scores; do not present the five replicates as independent validation sets or select the best seed. C0 and C1 are deterministic.

**Required diagnoses:** separate failure-density and runout-survival mean contributions for each fold that contains that event, plus macro failure means over three folds and macro runout means over **Wu and Matušů only** (Chen has no test runouts). Split test records at the **training fold's** minimum and maximum stress and show counts/mean NLL for in-range and out-of-range observations: Wu has seven below support, Matušů has 41 failures above support. Report numerical fit flags and any boundary parameters. Failure-only absolute error of predictive median `log10(N)` may be shown as descriptive; do not use it to choose an arm and do not calculate it on runouts. Romano's seven-row score and event breakdown are separately labelled transfer observations, never added to the primary macro.

**Interpretation rule set before fitting:** synthetic augmentation receives only a *preliminary positive* description if the five-seed mean S1 macro NLL is lower than **both** C1 and S0, at least two of three publication fold means improve versus C1, and neither the failure nor the runout macro mean NLL is higher than C1. Otherwise state the observed trade-off or absence of advantage. Show all seed-level results, including reversals and failures. With only three independent primary publications, do not attach a publication-level superiority p-value or claim Q1 readiness/algorithm novelty. A later genuinely untouched, original campaign and a method adding information beyond M1 self-sampling remain necessary to support a generalizable augmentation claim.

## Pre-score implementation clarification, 6 October 2026

Before any v2 fold scores were produced, two generator guard failures were identified: a finite latent lifetime beyond (10^{12}) cycles (correctly observed as a runout at (10^7)) and a latent draw below one cycle. The numerical generator now evaluates the censor event in log space, so an extremely long finite latent draw becomes the source's recorded (10^7)-cycle bound without exponentiation. It redraws a subcycle latent sample at the **same measured training stress and source offset** until (N \ge 1); the number of rejected subcycle draws is written per generated episode to the synthetic ledger. This conditions the teacher's synthetic distribution on physically countable lifetimes and does not change the real test rows, fold assignment, likelihood weights, or score. A nonfinite draw or more than 10,000 subcycle rejections invalidates that predeclared replicate and is reported. This clarification was locked before inspecting any v2 test score.
